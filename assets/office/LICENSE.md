# The Office's art: licence

The tiles, furniture and characters in `atlas.png` are original work for Workbench. They are not a copy of any
product's art and no third-party pack is used: every pixel is drawn by `scripts/office-art.mjs` in this repository,
which is the source of truth (the PNG is its output and is regenerated from it).

They may be used, changed and shipped with Workbench. If this art is ever replaced by a CC0 pack, that pack's
licence and its source belong in this file, written down as plainly as this.
